About AIML Department

Empowering Future Leaders in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized center of excellence in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning education, research, and innovation, producing professionals who drive technological advancement and societal impact.

Our Mission

  • Provide cutting-edge curriculum aligned with industry needs
  • Foster research culture in AI, ML, Deep Learning, and Data Science
  • Develop industry-ready professionals through hands-on projects
  • Promote ethical AI practices and responsible innovation
  • Collaborate with industry and academia for knowledge exchange
